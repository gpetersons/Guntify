Your phone is the connection.

Keep Guntify desktop mode running on your phone and leave both devices on the same router. The computer may use Wi-Fi or Ethernet.

Locking the phone is fine. If it disconnects when idle, open Guntify's app settings on Android and choose Battery → Unrestricted / Don't optimize. Keep the phone plugged in for long sessions.

Guest Wi-Fi, VPNs and router client isolation can prevent a connection. If the phone changes networks, restart desktop mode and use its new address.

Audio is played by this browser. The browser queue is separate from the phone queue. Some audio formats may not be supported by your browser. A browser must be paired with the one-time PIN shown on the phone. The local HTTP connection is not encrypted, so use trusted private Wi-Fi only. Stop desktop mode to revoke all browser sessions.
