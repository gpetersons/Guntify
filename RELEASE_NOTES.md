# 🎶 USpot 1.1 — Your music, your way

**Build 71** is the latest maintenance release. New to USpot? Here’s what’s waiting for you. 👇

## 🎧 Pick your next favorite

- **🏠 A Home that learns your taste** — discover albums and playlists based on what you actually listen to in USpot. Songs count toward personalization after 30 seconds of listening.
- **📻 Your own radio stations** — start with one song, or blend up to five into a Radio Mix. Choose Stay close, Balanced or Explore more to set the mood.
- **✨ A little surprise in your shuffle** — Smart Shuffle can mix relevant discoveries into your queue.
- **👀 Keep up with your artists** — explore new releases and popular tracks in Following.
- **📊 See your listening story** — browse your top songs, artists and local history in Stats.
- **📥 Music for later** — download songs manually, or turn on optional Smart Downloads in Settings. Smart Downloads stays off until you enable it.
- **🎛️ Make it sound like you** — equalizer presets and manual controls, plus light/dark themes that can follow your device.
- **🚗 Take your music along** — Android Auto support, including recent-radio browsing on compatible setups.

## ⬇️ Ready to press play?

**[Download USpot.apk](https://github.com/rare621/USpot/releases/download/v1.1-build-71/USpot.apk)** from this release and install it on Android 8.0 or newer.

**Already have USpot?** Back up your data in Settings, then install over your current app. **Don’t uninstall first** — that can erase local history and settings.

The source ZIP below is for anyone who wants to explore the code; you only need the APK to install the app. 👍

## 🛠️ A quick heads-up

Spotify account features, online streams and car controls depend on the services and devices you use. Intermittent stream-lookup problems are still being investigated. If playback gets stuck, save the playback diagnostics from Settings when reporting the problem — leave out passwords and tokens.
